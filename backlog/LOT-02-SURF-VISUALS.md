# Lot 02 — Visible Surf mount polish

Deferred scope for Blastoise, Tentacruel, Gyarados and Lapras:

- species-specific rider offsets by facing direction;
- floating height and water-contact tuning;
- movement animation and wake/splash effects;
- cleaner start/stop transitions and cries;
- robust battle/map restoration;
- Surfing Pikachu compatibility in Yellow;
- fallback to the native Surf sprite when an asset is unavailable.

Gameplay must remain entirely native: no custom water collision, music or movement rules.
