## Bundled PokePC follower sprites

The files under `assets/pokepc_followers/` were imported from the maintained
[`mfrtechconsult/PokePCFollowers`](https://github.com/mfrtechconsult/PokePCFollowers)
fork so Dramatic Sky Ride can retain a sandbox-safe 2D mount fallback without
requiring another mod to be active.

The Generation II sheets (National Pokédex 152–251) retain the provenance
documented by that project: Wilds of Kanto, whose follower assets trace back
through Followers EX, PokéPC, ShockSlayer and Pokémon Crystal Clear. The
remaining sheets retain the attribution and rights status of their source
